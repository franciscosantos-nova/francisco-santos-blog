Imagine someone who lives in the Algarve. More specifically in a sea view
flat at Praia da Rocha, which is worth a few hundred thousand euros. I
can't buy it, of course. But what if I could buy 1/1000 of it, in
seconds, from my phone, and sell that piece tomorrow if I need the money?

So, there is the point: that piece would be a **Token**, and the flat
would be a **Real World Asset**.

## What is a Real World Asset?

A Real World Asset (RWA) is anything that exists outside the blockchain
and has value in the traditional economy — for example, gold, a loan, or
a building.

## The Token is not the asset

What the Token carries is a **claim** on the asset, backed by legal
agreements in the real world.

> The Token is not the asset. It's a claim on the asset, backed by legal
> agreements in the real world.

## Not all assets are equal

Simpler assets have moved faster: government debt, private credit, gold,
commodities, invoices, carbon credits. Although I gave the example of the
flat, real estate is not where most of the action is — property law
makes flats hard to tokenize.
